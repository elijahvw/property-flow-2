## User outcomes and sources

- **PF-13/19 maintenance conversations:** residents reply to their own request in its existing update timeline. The team can keep private notes separate from requester-visible updates; safe author labels and management in-app reply notices reuse the real request, audit and notification sources. Text replies only. [Contract](maintenance-conversations.md).
- **PF-15/18 shared documents:** management explicitly shares one cleared lease file version with a named eligible resident. Resident list/download, optional receipt confirmation, revocation and immutable action history recheck current access. A receipt does not sign a document. New versions remain private until shared. [Contract](resident-document-sharing.md).
- **PF-23 saved tasks:** supported new-lease, renewal and inspection tasks save allowlisted fields to the current user's company account. Resume survives refresh/sign-in; 30-day expiry, version checks, source freshness and stable final submission keys protect against stale review and duplicate work. Files and other editor forms retain their existing clearly labelled scope. Navigation protection also covers browser history and programmatic route changes. [Contract](task-drafts.md).
- **PF-21 acquisition:** availability separates lease-derived vacancy/current/upcoming leases, readiness and owner-controlled rental listings. Missing reviews stay visible. All units default to Not listed for rent; vacancy or Ready never lists them. Only a current owner can explicitly list or remove a listing, including a future rental with a current lease visible. Inquiry/contact preference and tour/follow-up records lead to a manually recorded application, reasoned review and approval, then existing/prepared account + tenant contact + a real linked draft lease. Normal household approval, activation, invitation, billing and deposits follow from the lease workspace. [Contract](vacancy-applications.md).

Owner/manager application and draft APIs reuse fresh lease-management authority, explicit company filters and transaction-local RLS. Resident documents and replies use current source/participant/property eligibility. Staff assignment never grants lease, document, application or financial authority. Source writes and consequential audit/notification events commit atomically; retries retain the original action identity.
