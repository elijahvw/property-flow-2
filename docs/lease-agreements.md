## Preparing and reviewing a package

Open Lease → Agreements to search preparation titles, filter active/all or exact preparation status, and page through complete scoped results. Management may prepare up to 100 retained packages per lease. Each package has a title, lease/renewal/addendum/other purpose, private preparation notes, up to 10 exact document versions, up to 20 intended leaseholder signers and an intended company signer. There is one selected version per document. Agreement wording comes from the uploaded originals; the application does not generate contract terms or claim that a selected document matches the recorded lease automatically.

Save agreement version deliberately records an unsigned business preparation and an immutable revision. This is distinct from Save draft & close, which retains only an unsubmitted editor in memory on the current page. Editing a form never silently saves it. Changed input and structural document-selection edits trigger closing choices; explicit discard clears that editor. A lost-response submission freezes its original payload/key and keeps separate page-only recovery until retried. Reloading or leaving the lease clears unsubmitted editor drafts. Browser storage never receives agreement notes, originals or intended-signer data.

The complete management workflow is In preparation → Submit package for review → Awaiting review → Approve preparation with a note → Reviewed · unsigned. A reviewer can request changes with a reason instead. Reopen a reviewed or cancelled preparation with a reason to make changes. Cancel a preparing, awaiting or reviewed package explicitly; cancellation retains every version and does not claim to cancel a provider envelope. Management may prepare and review its own package; this increment does not impose a separate-reviewer rule. A review action uses the frozen saved version and cannot replace its files, notes or intended signers.

## Source records and original retention

Every saved package snapshots the exact lease terms/version, selected document-version metadata, intended leaseholder accounts and intended company signer. Document links open the real lease Documents section and retained versions; original bytes are never copied into agreement records or exposed by agreement APIs. Downloads use the existing authenticated document workflow with fresh exact-source authority and the shared available-status allowlist.

Preparation can retain an unavailable or archived original while work is incomplete. Submission and approval require at least one selected document, one eligible intended leaseholder, an active owner/manager company signer, unarchived documents and exact selected originals in `unscanned`, historical `pending` or historical `clean` status. New/pending files display **Not scanned**; no malware safety verdict is implied. Rejected/unknown states remain blockers. Restore an archived document before review. A newer unavailable original does not erase an earlier available version; management can deliberately select that retained version while the latest upload/status remains visible. Review never silently advances to a replacement original.

Lease changes, household version changes, selected account/name/email or household-participation role changes, company signer changes, archive/restore, newer uploads and recorded file-status changes invalidate the saved source comparison. Submission and approval re-resolve current sources within the authorized transaction and refuse a changed source or readiness blocker. The report displays the retained snapshot alongside current records, with an explicit out-of-date notice for an earlier approval. Request changes or reopen, correct selections and save a new version before review. Earlier approval snapshots remain intact.

Intended leaseholders come from explicit nonremoved leaseholder records and linked existing accounts; a legacy primary leaseholder is offered only when there is no explicit household record overriding that primary. Occupants, removed residents, inactive accounts and foreign-company memberships are excluded. Account identity is selected by existing account/record IDs; email equality never creates or merges identities. A primary-only choice is bound to that exact account, so changing the lease’s primary requires an explicit new signer selection. Portal approval, property assignment and first sign-in remain independent. An unverified account email is shown as unverified and may be prepared without fabricating verification or sending an invitation. Company signer choices are current active company owners/managers. These selections identify intended recipients only; they grant no portal or signing authority and perform no signature verification.

## Security, recovery and operations

Every context/list/detail/history/write/replay requires fresh `lease.manage`, active company membership, exact lease/company filters and the existing authorization fence with transaction-local RLS context. Platform authority, residency and staff maintenance execution grant no preparation access. Management-only notes and intended signer details are not added to resident/staff projections. Denied writes clear the open preparation/report; denied reads discard their resource data. Lease-section changes, source-document navigation and reauthentication retain bounded agreement search/filter/page/selection context.

Migration039 follows migration038 and adds LeaseAgreement, AgreementRevision and AgreementFile; its original-version foreign key uses the retained document identity introduced by 038. Composite source/company foreign keys retain exact original references; revisions and links are immutable with restricted runtime grants. A consequential save/review/cancel/reopen checks an opening version and durable submission receipt. Retries recover exactly one committed revision and audit; mismatched payloads, stale writes and racing reviews fail explicitly. Every package/revision/file link/audit writes atomically. There is no new cloud service or always-on resource, no lease/financial mutation, no account creation, no invitation and no provider call.

History pages contain 25 immutable versions with older/latest navigation. Search/status counts precede directory pagination. `agreement.save` and `agreement.review` are closed browser operation names for bounded outcomes/durations; existing authenticated API route metrics cover reads/writes. No notes, document metadata, source/signer IDs or query values enter telemetry. On 409, preserve the opening form, inspect current source/history and deliberately discard/reopen or return to preparation before a different save. On 403/404, recheck current management membership and exact source scope. Resolve unavailable/archive blockers through the real Documents source; never relabel a rejection or historical verdict. An audit failure must leave the package, original links, revision and receipt unchanged before retrying the original submission.

Original local validation covered real PostgreSQL migration/RLS/composite keys, source/role/CSRF boundaries, incomplete and then-quarantined preparation, duplicate and concurrent retries, stale/racing reviews, source invalidation, cancelled/reopened history, atomic audit rollback, complete paging, fresh revocation/suspension, unchanged financial/invitation/access records, and a real browser draft/recovery/review/source-return/phone journey. New retirement/available-file validation is pending separately in the reconciliation record. Hosted rollout, real-role acceptance, signature provider selection/activation and all actual signature lifecycle work remain separate.

Recorded leaseholder identity is independent of company role: an active same-company staff/manager/owner account remains an intended leaseholder signer. A company-role change alone does not invalidate that party snapshot. Disabled accounts/memberships and foreign-company memberships remain ineligible; company representative choices still require current management membership. Regression coverage includes primary and additional recorded leaseholders across all three roles and the disabled/foreign boundaries.
