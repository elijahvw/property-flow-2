### PF-20 · Cash as recorded by a cutoff, with comparison periods

The report uses existing immutable `LedgerEntry` monetary/source fields, `createdAt` and first `voidedAt`, alongside the business `occurredOn` date. It introduces no financial posting, replacement cash record or migration.

The operator selects an inclusive business-date period, an optional comparison period and an **as recorded through** date. Both periods use the same recording cutoff: the exclusive start of the next day in the company's stored timezone, computed by PostgreSQL so daylight-saving changes are respected. Entries created after that instant are absent even if their business date is earlier. A void recorded before the cutoff is visible as a correction and excluded from totals; a later void or its reason is not retroactively revealed in the earlier report.

Income, expenses, rent refunds, security-deposit receipts and deposit returns remain distinct. Exact integer-cent totals, groups, differences and complete CSV source rows use the existing cash classification. The report rejects an overlarge result instead of silently truncating money. Company/property labels are explicitly current labels; this is not reconstructed historical lease status, accrual accounting, profit, a tax statement or bank reconciliation.

Only the company's authorized owner/manager with finance authority can use it. Fresh company access, the reports/finances feature controls, explicit property filters and transaction-local RLS remain in force. Staff, residents and platform-only authority receive no customer cash access. Source links return to actual retained records; a historical report does not change their current state.

Next complete workflow: filter → generate cutoff/comparison → reconcile totals to included original sources → export → follow a source and return. Remaining PF-20 work includes historical billed/allocation/lease state, reconciled portfolio statements, opening balances and period controls.

Occupancy derives from recorded lease dates/status; operational readiness is a separately reviewed fact. No lease slot never becomes a claim of physical vacancy by itself.

The management view distinguishes occupied, future occupancy, confirmed ready, turnover, unavailable, unconfirmed and archived units. Planning dates describe a view of current source records, not an immutable reconstruction of what the operator knew historically. Future lease dates remain visible. Owners/managers can review a unit's condition and optional available date, provide a reason, and save against the opening version with a durable submission key.
