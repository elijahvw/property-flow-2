## Lifecycle and data protection

Migration056 adds nullable blueprint, predecessor and predecessor-version metadata to `SalesDemoSetup`. Existing rows are unchanged. A foreign key retains the original generation; a partial unique index prevents two active successors for one predecessor. Explicitly retired replacements, including intentionally retired incomplete setups, retain their lineage and release the active reservation. Leaving a tab or letting an operator lease expire does not release a pending replacement; finish or retire it before a later reviewed rebuild.

A blueprint-bound setup records its fixture, business day and application/identity context before provider work. Read-only plans contain no password or credential payload. A rebuild stores the reviewed source hash and replacement identity before retiring anything. Source/company/member/identity context is validated again under fresh platform authorization in the retirement transaction; provider calls remain outside database transactions. New source/audit failure rolls back the whole replacement graph while retaining the reservation and exact owned identities for retry. The predecessor's posted sources and audit history are not rewritten.

The existing pending/retiring setup also reserves its exact generated subjects through provider completion. Privileged reactivation, company reassignment/access grants, support enablement and invitation acceptance read that reservation after the same target User write fence used by retirement. Company-scoped mutations validate their ordinary target scope/eligibility first, preserving indistinguishable foreign/missing responses. A deterministic regression pauses the provider after its local ownership callback commits, proves reactivation/transfer/support enablement are rejected, verifies those foreign/missing responses, completes unrelated customer administration during that pause, and then releases cleanup. No database transaction spans provider HTTP. Ready/retired generations and no-setup legacy presenters retain their normal administration paths; a failed cleanup keeps its reservation until safely retried.

If a pending setup's day/template has changed, its captured plan is not silently updated. Retire the owned unfinished generation and review a fresh one. If source state changes after replacement reservation, the original is left unchanged by that attempt; retire the incomplete replacement before starting another review. These explicit recovery paths preserve both deterministic retries and business-history integrity.

The environment guard allows only declared development/test contexts (or the disposable runner), with a configured demo identity capability. The platform-authorized blueprint remains readable without a provider connection. Stored application origin and identity issuer must match before future provider work/cleanup. No setting, secret or environment has been changed as part of implementation.
